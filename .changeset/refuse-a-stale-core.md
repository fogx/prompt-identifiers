Refuse a `prompt-identifiers` too old to serve the middleware, where the middleware is built. A peer range is a warning rather than a wall, and a CJS `require` of a core without `findPlaceholders` reads it as `undefined` instead of failing to link, so a consumer with no `onDecode` listener ran a whole billed turn on a core that lowercases every identifier.
