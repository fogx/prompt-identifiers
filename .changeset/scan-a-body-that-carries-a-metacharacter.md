Match a placeholder body rather than compile it. The stripped-delimiter scan built its pattern from the body the format writes, so a formatter that writes its index twice around a literal, such as `(i) => \`id(${i})-${i}\``, threw `SyntaxError`out of`wrapGenerate`after the turn was paid for, and one writing a`.` widened the pattern into a warning about ordinary text.
