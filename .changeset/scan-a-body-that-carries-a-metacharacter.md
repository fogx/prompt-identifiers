Match a placeholder body rather than compile it. The stripped-delimiter scan built its pattern from the body the format writes, so a formatter that writes its index twice around a literal, such as one returning `id(0)-0`, threw a `SyntaxError` out of `wrapGenerate` after the turn was paid for, and one writing a `.` widened the pattern into a warning about ordinary text.
